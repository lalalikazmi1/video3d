Why do you suddenly want to clean your room the moment you sit down to study? It's not because you love cleaning. Psychologists Fuschia Sirois and Timothy Pychyl say procrastination isn't a time-management problem. It's a mood-management problem. When a task feels boring, hard, or uncertain, it creates a small, unpleasant feeling. And your brain wants that feeling gone. Right now. So it reaches for anything that feels better. Your phone. A snack. Suddenly, your messy room. Your brain isn't choosing the room. It's escaping the feeling. So here's the trick. Make the first step so small it doesn't feel bad. Don't write the essay.
