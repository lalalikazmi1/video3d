What if MrBeast gave you one million dollars… but you could never tell anyone?

Hour one. You're fine. Totally normal. You buy a sandwich. With guac.

Hour three. Your best friend says, "Bro, why are you smiling like that?"

Day two. There's a brand new car in your driveway. Under a blanket. Your mum asks. You say it's a very big cat.

Day four. You can't sleep. Scientists say people keeping a big secret think about it all the time, even when nobody's asking. So, yeah. You're cooked.

Day five. You crack. You whisper it to the only one you trust. Your dog.

Day six. Somehow… the whole school knows.

And then MrBeast steps out from behind a bush.

"You told the dog."
