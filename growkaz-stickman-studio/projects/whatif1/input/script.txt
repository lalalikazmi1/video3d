What if you could never forget anything? Every birthday. Every conversation. Every embarrassing moment. Forever, in perfect detail. Sounds like a superpower, right? For Jill Price, it's real. In 2006, scientists described her as one of the first people with a condition called highly superior autobiographical memory. Give her any date, and she can tell you what day of the week it was, and what she did. But she doesn't call it a gift. She's described it as nonstop, uncontrollable, and totally exhausting. Every argument stays fresh. Every regret replays in full colour. Because forgetting isn't a flaw. In 2017, neuroscientists Blake Richards and Paul Frankland argued that the brain forgets on purpose, to clear out the noise and help us move on. So maybe forgetting is the real superpower.
