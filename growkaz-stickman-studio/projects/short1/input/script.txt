Would you rather sit alone with your thoughts for fifteen minutes, or give yourself an electric shock? Easy, right? In 2014, psychologists at the University of Virginia asked people to do exactly that. No phone. No book. Just a plain room, and a button that gave a painful shock. These people had already felt the shock, and said they'd pay money to avoid it. And yet, two thirds of the men pressed it. A quarter of the women did too. One man pressed it one hundred and ninety times. They chose pain, just to escape their own minds. So next time you reach for your phone for no reason, ask yourself: what am I escaping?
