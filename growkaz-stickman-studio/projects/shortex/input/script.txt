It's been months. So why can't you stop thinking about your ex? In 2010, anthropologist Helen Fisher and her team put people who had just been dumped into a brain scanner. Then they showed them a photo of their ex. The parts of the brain that lit up weren't just linked to sadness. They were linked to reward, craving, and addiction. The same areas that light up when people crave drugs. That's why you check their profile. Reread old messages. Hope they'll text. It's not weakness. Your brain is going through withdrawal. And like any withdrawal, every little "hit" keeps the craving alive. So the fastest way through is the hardest. No checking. No scrolling. No "just one look".
