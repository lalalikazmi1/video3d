A fire lieutenant leads his crew into a burning house. They spray water on the kitchen. But the fire won't die. Something feels wrong. The room is too hot. Too quiet. He can't explain why, but he yells, "Everybody out!" Seconds later, the floor they were standing on collapses. The fire was never in the kitchen. It was burning in the basement, right under their feet. His gut saved every life in that room. So should you always trust your gut? Here's the uncomfortable truth. Sometimes your gut is a genius. And sometimes, it's a con artist. The trick is knowing which one is talking.
