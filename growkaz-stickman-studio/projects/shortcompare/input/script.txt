Why do bronze medal winners look happier than silver? In 1995, psychologists studied footage of Olympic athletes on the podium. The silver medallists, second best in the entire world, looked less happy than the people who came third. Why? Because of who they compared themselves to. Silver thinks: I almost won gold. Bronze thinks: I almost got nothing. Same podium. Completely different feeling. And you do this every day. Psychologist Leon Festinger said we judge ourselves by comparing ourselves to others. The problem is, on social media you're comparing yourself to everyone's gold medal moments. So if you want to feel better, change who you compare yourself to.
