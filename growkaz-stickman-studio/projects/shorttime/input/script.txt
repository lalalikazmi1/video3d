Why does two hours with friends feel like ten minutes, but ten minutes in a queue feels like an hour? It comes down to attention. When you're bored, you keep checking the clock. And the more you pay attention to time, the slower it seems to move. When you're having fun, you stop watching the clock, so time seems to disappear. But here's the strange part. Writer and psychologist Claudia Hammond calls it the holiday paradox. A fun, new week flies by while you're in it. But when you look back, it feels long, because it's packed with new memories. A boring week drags while you're in it, but looking back, it almost vanishes. So if the years are flying by, don't slow down. Do something new. Follow for more psychology.
