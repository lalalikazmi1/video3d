What if you never felt fear again? No nerves before an exam. No anxiety. No panic. Sounds perfect, right? There's a woman known to scientists only as S.M. A rare condition damaged a small part of her brain called the amygdala, and she can't feel fear. Researchers took her to an exotic pet store. She walked straight up to the snakes and tried to touch them. They had to stop her. They took her to a haunted house. She laughed at the monsters, and even scared one of the actors. But in real life, she's walked into dangerous situations that most people would run from. Because fear isn't the enemy. It's an alarm system that's kept humans alive for millions of years.
