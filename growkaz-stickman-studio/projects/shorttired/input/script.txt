You did nothing today. So why are you exhausted? You stayed in bed. You scrolled. You watched three episodes of something you don't even like. And somehow, you feel more tired than after a busy day. Here's why. Psychologist Robert Kurzban and his colleagues argue that mental tiredness isn't really your brain running out of fuel. It's a signal. Your brain is telling you: this isn't worth it. Do something else. So when your day is full of things that don't matter to you, that signal never switches off. Boredom feels like tiredness. And lying still makes it worse, because your body follows your mind. The fix isn't more rest. It's doing something that actually matters to you. Even a short walk. Follow for more psychology.
