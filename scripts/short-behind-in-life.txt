Everyone your age seems to have it all figured out. The job. The relationship. The life. Except you. Why do you feel so behind? In the 1960s, psychologist Bernice Neugarten described something she called the social clock. It's an invisible timetable in your head, that tells you when you should finish school, find a partner, buy a house, have it all together. When you're off that clock, it feels like failure. But here's the thing. That clock isn't real. It was made up by the people around you. And online, you only see everyone's best moments, never their struggles. Vera Wang didn't design her first dress until she was forty. Colonel Sanders started franchising his chicken in his sixties. You're not behind. You're on your own clock. Follow for more psychology.
