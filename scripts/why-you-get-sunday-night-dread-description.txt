It's Sunday. Six p.m. The weekend isn't over, nothing bad has happened, and yet something in your chest starts to tighten. Tomorrow is Monday. Why does a day that hasn't even happened yet ruin the one you're actually living? Here's the psychology of the Sunday scaries, and why Monday is almost never as bad as your brain says it will be.

In this video we explore the brain-scan study where people chose a bigger electric shock just to stop waiting, why your brain can't tell a predator from a Monday inbox, anticipatory anxiety, the Zeigarnik effect and why unfinished tasks nag you on Sunday night, the research showing the "Monday blues" are mostly a myth, the impact bias, and your psychological immune system. Then: five ways to get your Sunday back.
