A firefighter pulled his crew out of a burning house seconds before the floor collapsed. He couldn't explain why. So should YOU trust your gut?

Sometimes your gut is a genius. Sometimes it's a con artist. In this video we break down what psychology actually says about intuition: Daniel Kahneman's fast and slow thinking, Gary Klein's research on firefighters, the famous bat-and-ball puzzle, and why expert predictions often do little better than chance. By the end you'll have two simple questions to tell when your gut is worth listening to.
