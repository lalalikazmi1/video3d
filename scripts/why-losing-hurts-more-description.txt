Would you take a coin flip where you win $150 or lose $100? The odds are in your favour, yet most people say no. Here's the psychology behind why losing hurts more than winning feels good.

Loss aversion is one of the most powerful forces in your mind. In this video we explore Kahneman and Tversky's Prospect Theory, the famous coffee mug experiment, how one word can flip a life-or-death decision, why marketers say "don't miss out", and how even the world's best golfers fight harder not to lose. Then: three simple ways to stop loss aversion from making your choices for you.
