She studied her attacker's face so she would never forget it. She picked him out of a lineup and swore she was 100% sure. She was wrong. Here's the psychology of why your memory lies to you, and why you never feel it happen.

Memory isn't a video recording. Every time you remember something, your brain rebuilds it. In this video we explore the true story of Jennifer Thompson and Ronald Cotton, Bartlett's "War of the Ghosts" retellings, Loftus and Palmer's famous car-crash experiment, how a false childhood memory was planted in the lab, what the Challenger disaster revealed about "flashbulb" memories, and why confidence is not the same as accuracy. Then: three simple ways to protect yourself from your own memory.
