What if you could read everyone's mind for one day? You'd finally know what they really think of you. Your crush. Your boss. The people who saw you trip this morning. So you walk into a room, and you listen. And here's what you'd probably hear. Nothing about you. In one famous study at Cornell University, students wore an embarrassing T-shirt into a room, and guessed half the people would notice. Only about a quarter did. Psychologists call it the spotlight effect. And it gets better. Studies on the illusion of transparency show we massively overestimate how much others can see our nerves, our awkwardness, our panic. So if you could read minds, you'd discover the most freeing truth of all. Everyone is too busy worrying about themselves. Follow for more psychology.
